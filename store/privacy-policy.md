# Privacy Policy — Don't Break the Chain

**Last updated: 8 September 2026**

Don't Break the Chain ("the app", "the game") is published by **Opira
Solutions** ("we", "us"). This policy explains what the app does and does not
collect. It applies to the Android app with the package name
`com.opirasolutions.dontbreakthechain`.

## The short version

The game itself collects nothing. It has no account, no sign-in and no server.
Everything you do — levels cleared, stars, times, streaks, achievements and
settings — is stored only on your own device and is never sent to us.

The one exception is advertising: the game shows ads from Google AdMob, and the
Google Mobile Ads SDK collects a device advertising identifier in order to serve
them.

## What is stored on your device

- Level progress, stars and best times
- Daily Challenge results and your streak
- Achievements
- Sound, haptics and reduced-motion settings
- Hint balance

This data lives in the app's local storage. We cannot see it. Uninstalling the
app deletes it. If you have Android backup enabled, Google may include this data
in your device backup under Google's own terms.

## Advertising

The app displays interstitial and rewarded video ads supplied by **Google
AdMob**. To serve and measure those ads, the Google Mobile Ads SDK may collect
and process:

- your device's **advertising ID** (a resettable identifier)
- coarse device and app information, such as device model, OS version and app
  version
- ad interaction events, such as whether an ad was shown, viewed or clicked
- an IP address, from which an approximate (city-level) location can be inferred

This data is collected by Google, not by us. We never receive it in a form that
identifies you. We do not collect, store or share your name, email address,
contacts, photos, files, precise location or any other personal information.

Google's use of this data is governed by:

- Google Privacy Policy — https://policies.google.com/privacy
- How Google uses information from sites or apps that use our services —
  https://policies.google.com/technologies/partner-sites

### Your choices

- **Reset or delete your advertising ID**: Android **Settings → Privacy → Ads**.
  From there you can reset the identifier or delete it entirely, which stops
  personalised advertising.
- **Users in the EEA, the UK and Switzerland**: on first launch the app shows a
  Google-provided consent form where you can accept or refuse personalised ads.
  If you refuse, or if consent is unavailable, the app requests
  **non-personalised** ads instead. You can change this choice at any time by
  clearing the app's data or reinstalling.

## Children

The app is not directed at children under 13. It is rated for a general
audience, requests only general-audience ad content, and is not enrolled in
Google Play's Designed for Families programme. We do not knowingly collect
personal information from children.

## Data sharing and selling

We do not sell, rent or trade any data. We do not share any data with third
parties beyond what the Google Mobile Ads SDK collects and processes as
described above.

## Data retention and deletion

We hold no data about you, so there is nothing for us to retain or delete.
To remove everything the app has stored, delete the app's data in Android
**Settings → Apps → Don't Break the Chain → Storage → Clear data**, or uninstall
the app.

## Permissions the app requests

| Permission | Why |
| --- | --- |
| `INTERNET` | fetch ads |
| `ACCESS_NETWORK_STATE` | check connectivity before requesting an ad |
| `AD_ID` | read the advertising ID so ads can be served |
| `VIBRATE` | haptic feedback on taps (can be turned off in Settings) |

The game is fully playable with no network connection.

## Security

The app makes no network requests of its own. All ad traffic from the Google
Mobile Ads SDK is encrypted in transit (HTTPS).
